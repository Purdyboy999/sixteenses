Cara membaca tabel:

  • Baris He/She/It (tunggal) selalu pakai: is, does, has, was, did, had

  • has/have, had → membentuk Perfect Tense

1. Penggunaan To Be (is / am / are / was / were)

To Be wajib ada di kalimat nominal (predikatnya bukan kata kerja), dan juga muncul di beberapa tenses tertentu (Continuous dan Passive).

SubjectPresentPast
Iamwas
You / We / Theyarewere
He / She / Itiswas

a. Sebagai predikat utama (kalimat nominal)

Digunakan ketika predikat berupa Noun, Adjective, atau Adverb — bukan kata kerja.

  • I am a student. (noun)
  • She is beautiful. (adjective)
  • They were at school. (adverb of place)

b. Sebagai kata kerja bantu (auxiliary)

Digunakan bersama Verb-ing (Continuous) atau Verb-3 (Passive).

  • She is reading a book now.
  • The letter was written by him.
Kesalahan Umum Pemula

❌ She is go to school. — To Be tidak boleh langsung diikuti V1

✅ She is going to school. (To Be + V-ing)

✅ She goes to school. (tanpa To Be, pakai V1+s/es)

Aturan Penting: Dalam kalimat verbal simple, jangan gabungkan To Be dengan V1/V2 tanpa akhiran -ing atau -ed.

2. Aturan Penggunaan V1 / V1+s/es

V1 (bentuk dasar) digunakan dalam Simple Present Tense. Bentuknya berubah tergantung subject.

SubjectBentuk VerbContoh
I, You, We, TheyV1 (tanpa tambahan)I play, They eat
He, She, ItV1 + s/esShe plays, It eats
  • 1. Jika Verb berakhiran ss, sh, ch ,x , o, z maka verb ditambahkan +es :

    kiss → kisses

    wash → washes

    watch → watches

    box → boxes

    go → goes

    buzz → buzzes

  • 2. Jika Verb berakhiran konsonan + y maka huruf y dihilangkan lalu ditambahkan +ies :

    fly → flies

    cry → cries

  • 3. Jika tidak termasuk dalam kategori di atas, maka gunakan +s :

    buy → buys

    eat → eats

    drink → drinks

Kesalahan Umum Pemula

❌ She play badminton. → ✅ She plays badminton.

❌ He watchs TV. → ✅ He watches TV.

3. Subject-Verb Agreement

Bentuk kata kerja (atau To Be) harus sesuai dengan jenis subject-nya: tunggal atau jamak.

Subject Tunggal

Verb + s/es, atau To Be: is/was

The cat sleeps all day.
My brother is a doctor.

Subject Jamak

Verb tanpa s/es, atau To Be: are/were

The cats sleep all day.
My brothers are doctors.

SituasiAturanContoh
Subject + "and"Dianggap jamakTom and Jerry play football.
Subject + "or/nor"Verb ikut subject terdekatNeither the teacher nor the students were ready.
Uncountable nounDianggap tunggalThe information is correct.
everyone, somebody, each, eitherSelalu tunggalEveryone likes the movie.
news, mathematics, physicsDianggap tunggalThe news is shocking.
Kesalahan Umum Pemula

❌ Everyone are happy. → ✅ Everyone is happy.

❌ My parents is at home. → ✅ My parents are at home.

4. Penggunaan Do / Does / Did

Dipakai di kalimat verbal untuk membuat bentuk negatif dan tanya pada Simple Present & Simple Past. Setelah do/does/did, verb selalu kembali ke V1.

SubjectSimple PresentSimple Past
I / You / We / Theydodid
He / She / Itdoesdid

(-) She does not play badminton.

(?) Do you like coffee?

(-) They did not go to school yesterday.

(?) Did he finish his homework?

❌ She does not plays badminton. → ✅ She does not play badminton.

5. Penggunaan Has / Have / Had

Dipakai sebagai auxiliary untuk membentuk Perfect Tense, selalu diikuti V3 (Past Participle).

SubjectPresent PerfectPast Perfect
I / You / We / Theyhavehad
He / She / Ithashad

She has finished her homework. (has + V3)

I have visited Bali twice. (have + V3)

They had left before I arrived. (had + V3)

Catatan: has/have/had juga bisa berarti "punya/memiliki" (kata kerja biasa) — beda dari fungsi auxiliary Perfect Tense.

Ringkasan Cepat (Cheat Sheet)

  • To Be wajib jika predikat bukan kata kerja, dan sebagai auxiliary V-ing/V3.
  • V1 + s/es hanya untuk He/She/It di Simple Present.
  • Subject-Verb Agreement: tunggal → verb+s/is/was/does/has, jamak → verb polos/are/were/do/have.
  • Do/Does/Did untuk negatif & tanya kalimat verbal, diikuti V1.
  • Has/Have/Had untuk Perfect Tense, diikuti V3.

Kalimat Verbal & Nominal

Dua tipe kalimat dasar yang wajib dikuasai sebelum belajar tenses.

Kalimat Verbal

Kalimat Verbal adalah kalimat yang predikatnya berupa kata kerja utama (Ordinary Verb) yang menunjukkan suatu tindakan atau aktivitas.

Rumus: Subject + Verb + (Object/Complement)

Contoh:

  • She plays badminton every Sunday. (plays = verb)
  • They watched a movie last night. (watched = verb)
  • I will eat an apple. (will eat = verb)

Kalimat Nominal

Kalimat Nominal adalah kalimat yang predikatnya BUKAN kata kerja (Non-Verb). Predikat ini biasanya berupa Noun (kata benda), Adjective (kata sifat), atau Adverb (kata keterangan). Oleh karena itu, kalimat nominal wajib menggunakan To Be (is/am/are/was/were/been) sebagai kata kerja bantu.

Rumus: Subject + To Be + Complement (Noun/Adjective/Adverb)

Contoh:

  • She is smart. (is = to be, smart = adjective)
  • They were students. (were = to be, students = noun)
  • I am in the classroom. (am = to be, in the classroom = adverb)

Part of Speech

8 kelas kata dalam bahasa Inggris yang menyusun sebuah kalimat.

Terdapat 8 kelas kata dalam bahasa Inggris. Berikut adalah penjelasan rinci untuk masing-masing kelas kata:

Noun (Kata Benda)

Digunakan untuk menamai orang, tempat, benda, ide, atau konsep abstrak.

  • Common Noun: city, man, book
  • Proper Noun: Jakarta, John, Harry Potter
  • Abstract Noun: happiness, freedom, love

Verb (Kata Kerja)

Digunakan untuk menyatakan suatu tindakan (action) atau keadaan (state of being).

  • Action Verb: run, eat, write, jump
  • Linking Verb: is, am, are, look, seem
  • Helping Verb: have, do, will, can

Pronoun (Kata Ganti)

Digunakan untuk menggantikan kata benda (noun) untuk menghindari pengulangan yang tidak perlu.

  • Subjective: I, you, they, we, he, she, it
  • Objective: me, you, them, us, him, her, it
  • Possessive: mine, yours, theirs, ours, his, hers

Adverb (Kata Keterangan)

Digunakan untuk memodifikasi/menjelaskan Verb, Adjective, atau Adverb lainnya.

  • Adverb of Manner: quickly, slowly, happily
  • Adverb of Time: yesterday, now, soon
  • Adverb of Place: here, there, everywhere

Preposition (Kata Depan)

Digunakan sebelum noun/pronoun untuk menunjukkan arah, waktu, tempat, atau lokasi spasial.

Contoh: in, on, at, under, between, above, behind

She is sitting on the chair.

Conjunction (Kata Hubung)

Digunakan untuk menghubungkan kata, frasa, atau klausa agar menjadi satu kesatuan.

Contoh: and, but, or, so, because, although

I like tea but he likes coffee.

Interjection (Kata Seru)

Kata yang digunakan untuk mengekspresikan emosi, perasaan terkejut, atau reaksi mendadak.

Contoh: Wow!, Ouch!, Oops!, Yay!, Alas!

Wow! That is a beautiful painting.

Adjective (Kata Sifat) - Penjelasan Rinci

Adjective digunakan untuk menjelaskan atau memodifikasi Noun atau Pronoun. Adjective memberikan informasi lebih seperti sifat, jumlah, atau jenis.

Rumus Pembentukan Noun Phrase (Frasa Kata Benda)

Ketika membentuk Frasa Kata Benda, kita sering menggunakan urutan Adjective. Rumus yang sangat populer di kelas grammar (seperti di Kampung Inggris) adalah: La - Da - N

  • LA (Limiting Adjective): Kata sifat yang membatasi.
  • LA 1 — Article : The, A, An
  • LA 2 — Demonstrative Adjective : This, That These, Those
  • LA 3 — Possessive Adjective : My, Your, His, Her, Its, Our, Their
  • LA 4 — Indefinite quantity : Some, Any, Many, Much, Few, Little, Several
  • LA 5 — Numeral : 1, 2, 3 / first, second, third
  • DA (Descriptive Adjective): Kata sifat yang menjelaskan sifat Noun.
  • DA 1 — Character : Kind, Brave, Smart
  • DA 2 — Quality : expensive, cheap, good, bad
  • DA 3 — Size : Big, Small, Large, Tiny
  • DA 4 — Age : Old, Young, Adult
  • DA 5 — Temperature : Hot, Cold, Warm, Cool
  • DA 6 — Shape : Round, Square, Long, Short
  • DA 7 — Color : Red, Blue, Green, Yellow
  • DA 8 — Nationality : Indonesian, American, British, Javanese, Balinese

Contoh Penggunaan La-Da-N:

"The beautiful big red Indonesian table"

  • The (La 1 Article)
  • beautiful (Da 2 Quality)
  • big (Da 3 Size)
  • red (Da 7 Color)
  • Indonesian (Da 8 Nationality)
  • table (Noun)

16 Tenses Bahasa Inggris

Kuasai perubahan bentuk kata kerja berdasarkan waktu kejadian.

Modal Auxiliary & Semi-Modal

Kata kerja bantu untuk menyatakan kemampuan, izin, kemungkinan, keharusan, dan saran.

Catatan penempatan: Materi ini diletakkan setelah halaman "16 Tenses", karena modal sering dikombinasikan dengan pemahaman waktu (present/past) yang sudah dipelajari di tenses.

1. Definisi

Modal Auxiliary

Kata kerja bantu untuk menyatakan kemampuan, izin, kemungkinan, keharusan, atau saran. Selalu diikuti V1 (bare infinitive) — tanpa to, tanpa s/es, tanpa -ing.

Semi-Modal (Marginal Modal)

Kata kerja bantu yang fungsinya mirip modal, tapi berupa frasa (lebih dari satu kata) dan cara pemakaiannya sedikit berbeda — sebagian boleh berubah bentuk dan diikuti "to".

3. Aturan Modal dan Semi-Modal

Aturan Modal Auxiliary

  • Tidak berubah bentuk, meski subject He/She/It (tidak ada tambahan -s)
  • Selalu diikuti V1 — tidak boleh V1+s, V-ing, atau to+V1
  • Tidak boleh digabung dua modal sekaligus (❌ she will can go)
  • Negatif langsung tambahkan "not" setelah modal, tanpa do/does/did

Aturan Semi-Modal Auxiliary

  • Boleh berubah bentuk mengikuti subject (has to, is going to)
  • Diikuti "to" + V1, bukan langsung V1 (kecuali "used to")
  • Negatif/tanya sebagian pakai do/does/did (have to, need to, ought to)
  • Negatif/tanya sebagian pakai to be (be going to, be able to)
Contoh perbandingan:

✅ She must go. (Modal, langsung V1, tanpa to)

✅ She has to go. (Semi-Modal, pakai "to"+V1, berubah jadi "has")

4. Pola Rumus

Rumus Modal Auxiliary

  • (+) Subject + Modal + V1 + (Object/Complement)
  • (-) Subject + Modal + not + V1
  • (?) Modal + Subject + V1?

(+) She can swim.

(-) She cannot (can't) swim.

(?) Can she swim?

Rumus Semi-Modal Auxiliary

  • (+) Subject + Semi-Modal (to be/do/does/did bagian) + to + V1
  • (-) Subject + do/does/did/to be + not + Semi-Modal + to + V1
  • (?) Do/Does/Did/To be + Subject + Semi-Modal + to + V1?

Have to:

(+) She has to work.

(-) She does not have to work.

(?) Does she have to work?

Be going to:

(+) They are going to travel.

(-) They are not going to travel.

(?) Are they going to travel?

Perbandingan Penting: Must vs Have to

ModalSumber Keharusan
MustKeharusan dari pembicara sendiri (opini/perasaan pribadi)
Have toKeharusan dari aturan/keadaan eksternal

I must finish this today. (saya sendiri yang merasa harus)

I have to wear a helmet. (karena aturan, bukan keinginan sendiri)

Catatan penting: Must tidak punya bentuk lampau. Untuk keharusan di masa lalu, gunakan had to.

❌ Yesterday, I must go to the dentist.

✅ Yesterday, I had to go to the dentist.

Kesalahan Umum Pemula

❌ She can speaks French. → ✅ She can speak French.

❌ You should to study harder. → ✅ You should study harder.

❌ She has go to work. → ✅ She has to go to work.

❌ He musts go now. → ✅ He must go now.

Ringkasan Cepat (Cheat Sheet)

  • Modal (can, could, may, might, will, would, shall, should, must) → langsung diikuti V1, tanpa to, tidak berubah bentuk.
  • Semi-Modal (have to, need to, ought to, used to, be going to, be able to) → diikuti "to" + V1, boleh berubah bentuk mengikuti subject/tense.
  • Must vs Have to: must = opini pribadi, have to = aturan eksternal.
  • Must tidak punya bentuk lampau — pakai had to.
  • Can tidak punya bentuk future/perfect — pakai be able to.

Passive Voice

Kalimat di mana subject dikenai tindakan, bukan yang melakukan tindakan.

Catatan penempatan: Materi ini diletakkan setelah halaman "Modal Auxiliary", karena Passive Voice menggabungkan pemahaman To Be (dari materi pre-requisite) dan semua bentuk waktu di 16 Tenses.

1. Definisi

Passive Voice (kalimat pasif) adalah kalimat di mana subject dikenai suatu tindakan, bukan yang melakukan tindakan. Lawannya adalah Active Voice (kalimat aktif), di mana subject adalah pelaku tindakan.

Active Voice

Chef cooks the food.

(Chef = pelaku)

Passive Voice

The food is cooked by the chef.

(food = yang dikenai tindakan)

2. Rumus Dasar

  • Subject (yang dikenai) + To Be + V3 (Past Participle) + (by + pelaku)

Cara mengubah Active → Passive

  1. Object pada kalimat aktif menjadi subject pada kalimat pasif
  2. Verb diubah menjadi To Be + V3, To Be disesuaikan dengan tense aslinya
  3. Subject pada kalimat aktif dipindah ke belakang, ditambah "by" (boleh dihilangkan jika pelaku tidak penting/tidak diketahui)

Active: She writes a letter.

Passive: A letter is written by her.

3. Perubahan Aktif → Pasif di Setiap Tense

Bagian To Be menyesuaikan tense kalimat aslinya, V3 selalu tetap.

TenseRumus PassiveContoh
Simple PresentS + am/is/are + V3The room is cleaned every day.
Present ContinuousS + am/is/are + being + V3The room is being cleaned now.
Present PerfectS + have/has + been + V3The room has been cleaned.
Simple PastS + was/were + V3The room was cleaned yesterday.
Past ContinuousS + was/were + being + V3The room was being cleaned at 8 AM.
Past PerfectS + had + been + V3The room had been cleaned before I arrived.
Simple FutureS + will + be + V3The room will be cleaned tomorrow.
Future PerfectS + will + have + been + V3The room will have been cleaned by noon.
ModalS + Modal + be + V3The room must be cleaned.

4. Aturan Penting

  1. Hanya kalimat verbal (Transitive Verb) yang bisa diubah jadi pasif — verb harus punya object.
    ❌ He sleeps → tidak bisa dipasifkan (tidak ada object)
  2. V3 selalu tetap, tidak berubah menurut tense — yang berubah adalah To Be di depannya
  3. "By + pelaku" boleh dihilangkan jika pelaku tidak diketahui, tidak penting, atau sudah jelas dari konteks
    The window was broken (oleh siapa tidak disebutkan)
  4. Jika subject pada kalimat aktif adalah kata ganti, ubah ke bentuk objektif setelah "by"
    She helps me → I am helped by her (bukan "by she")
Kesalahan Umum Pemula

❌ The food is cook by her. → ✅ The food is cooked by her.

❌ The letter was write by him. → ✅ The letter was written by him.

❌ A letter is written by she. → ✅ A letter is written by her.

5. Fungsi/Kegunaan Passive Voice

Menekankan objek/hasil

Dipakai saat pelaku tidak penting atau tidak diketahui.

My phone was stolen.

Konteks formal/ilmiah

Laporan penelitian, berita, atau instruksi teknis.

The experiment was conducted in a controlled environment.

Menghindari menyalahkan langsung

Lebih sopan daripada menuding seseorang.

A mistake was made in the report.

Pelaku sudah jelas

Tidak perlu disebut berulang.

He was arrested yesterday.

Ringkasan Cepat (Cheat Sheet)

  • Rumus dasar: Subject + To Be + V3 + (by + pelaku)
  • To Be menyesuaikan tense, V3 selalu tetap
  • Hanya verb transitive (punya object) yang bisa dipasifkan
  • "By + pelaku" boleh dihilangkan jika tidak penting/tidak diketahui
  • Kata ganti setelah "by" harus bentuk objektif: me, you, him, her, it, us, them
